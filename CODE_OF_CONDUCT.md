# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Puzzle Genome Atlas is a collaborative research space. Participants must:

- discuss evidence and methods rather than attacking people;
- welcome corrections, counterexamples and negative results;
- distinguish disagreement from misconduct;
- credit sources and prior contributors;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation and sexualised conduct;
- respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.

## Research conduct

Deliberate fabrication, concealed conflicts of interest, plagiarism and knowingly unsupported novelty claims are unacceptable.

Good-faith errors are not misconduct. Contributors are expected to correct them transparently and preserve an auditable change record.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and
other spaces where a participant represents Puzzle Genome Atlas.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, reject participation or temporarily or permanently restrict contributors whose behaviour violates this code.

To report a concern, contact the repository owner privately through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue. If no private maintainer channel is available or immediate platform action is needed, use GitHub's Report Abuse mechanism.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith, kept as confidential as practical and handled without retaliation.
