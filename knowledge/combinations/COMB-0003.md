## Decision structure

The player risks exposing one cell in a fixed concealed field. Safe reveals
produce exact local counts, zero clues expand information automatically and
the accumulated equalities identify forced-safe and forced-hazard positions.
Exposing a hazard terminates the attempt, while success requires every safe
position to be revealed.

## Combination match boundary

A game does not instantiate this combination merely because it hides objects
or displays numbers. The hidden contents must remain fixed during play, safe
reveals must supply exact local aggregate constraints, zero clues must propagate
reveals, hazard exposure must be terminal and the objective must require the
safe set to be exposed.
