# Research Principles

1. **Evidence before invention.** Decompose existing systems before proposing
   finished games.
2. **Genes are typed and bounded.** A term is not an active gene until it has an
   ID, definition, boundary, evidence and analysed example.
3. **Separate control from response.** Player action, automatic behaviour,
   constraint, information, objective, time and presentation are different
   layers.
4. **Novelty must be checked externally.** Search games, historical catalogues,
   papers, game-jam projects and prototypes before claiming a gap.
5. **Canonical knowledge and research leads are different.** Unverified ideas
   remain under `research/`; accepted records live under `knowledge/`.
6. **Do not repeat conclusions.** New work must add evidence, genes,
   counterexamples, combinations, corrections or explicit negative results.
7. **Keep hypotheses falsifiable.** State rejection conditions, counterexamples
   and uncertainty.
8. **Compare the full corpus efficiently.** Scan every indexed signature, record
   exact matches centrally and write detailed prose only for nearest structural
   neighbours.
9. **Report absence honestly.** Write `New genes: none`, `New combinations:
   none` or `Taxonomy changes: none` when applicable.
10. **Make corrections auditable.** Classification changes require a numbered
    taxonomy-change record; architecture changes require an ADR.
11. **Sample for diversity.** Do not analyse two structurally close games in
    sequence when a higher-information subject is available.
12. **Broaden the corpus.** Include classical, mathematical, mechanical,
    historical, experimental and academic systems, not only mobile games.
13. **Player experience outranks formal elegance.** A rich state space can
    still be unreadable or uninteresting.
14. **Failure should teach.** Record whether players can trace failure to a
    decision or whether randomness and opacity dominate attribution.
15. **Preserve player trust.** Future prototypes should avoid artificial
    retention pressure, payment walls and punishment for absence.
16. **Challenge the plan openly.** If a higher-information sequence becomes
    available, propose it immediately and preserve displaced subjects as
    backlog.
17. **Prototype only after mapping.** Prototype the smallest candidate only
    after taxonomy, combination and novelty checks justify it.

## Evidence requirement

Every substantive claim uses the status, evidence-quality and confidence model
defined in [EVIDENCE_MODEL.md](EVIDENCE_MODEL.md). Puzzle Genome Atlas does not
use `Law`.
